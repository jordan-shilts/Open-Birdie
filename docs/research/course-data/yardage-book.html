Yardage Book

Hole-by-hole yardages, elevation, out of bounds, penalty areas and green data for the courses the sim features. Every figure links to where it came from. A dash means no published figure was found; nothing here is estimated.

Gathered from official scorecards, course and resort sites, USGA, R&A and PGA TOUR material, and golf publications, then checked: every tee's hole yardages add up to its published total. Elevation is tee-to-green change in feet, negative meaning downhill. Out of bounds (white stakes, walls, fences, property lines) is kept separate from penalty areas such as water, cliffs and gorse.
